<p class="label">Overview</p>

AthenaScribe helps school teams manage document translation and review so families can understand what comes next.

Through BRIDGEGOOD and partner organizations, our team explored education and opportunity gaps in the East Bay. We focused on how schools share information with families.

### Translation stops at the attachment.

A permission slip or health form can introduce another task: finding someone to translate it, check important information, and return it to the family.

## What if the document were as understandable as the message?

We saw an opportunity to connect document translation, staff review, and family access in one workflow.

### A shared workflow for school document translation.

AthenaScribe translates school documents and routes important ones to staff for review before families receive them.

### Translation introduced another question: who checks it?

School documents can include consent, health, and student information. I examined education policy and district guidance, then worked with the product lead to incorporate staff review into the proposed workflow.
